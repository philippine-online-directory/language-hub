# Philippine Online Dictionary
 
The Philippine Online Dictionary is a community-built dictionary for endangered Philippine languages. It gives speakers, learners and researchers one place to search for words, explore individual languages and add entries of their own.
 
https://www.philippineonlinedictionary.com/
 
## Why this exists
 
The Philippines is home to more than a hundred languages, and many of them are spoken by small and shrinking communities. When a language disappears, it takes with it words for ideas, practices and relationships that have no clean English equivalent. A translation can give you the closest English word, but it often loses the cultural meaning behind it.
 
This project records those words while people who speak them can still contribute, and keeps them freely available online.
 
## Languages
 
The dictionary currently documents languages including:
 
- Ibanag
- Ayta Mag-antsi
- Ivatan
- Itbayat
- Tagbanua
- Dumagat
 
## Features
 
**Search.** Look up words across every language in the dictionary.
 
**Language pages.** Each language has its own page for browsing its entries.
 
**Contributing entries.** Community members can submit new words and definitions, which is how the dictionary grows. Over a hundred contributors have added entries so far.
